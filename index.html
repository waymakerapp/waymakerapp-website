What WayMaker does

Everything in one place

A free tool built for people experiencing homelessness and those who support them, covering services across Eastbourne and Lewes.

🗺️
Live service map
Colour-coded markers show food banks, shelters, health services, and more across both towns.
📋
Services directory
Browse every service with opening times and contact details — even without a data connection.
📥
Offline digital map
Download the East Sussex Homelessness Services chart for offline use — no signal needed.
🔔
SWEP notifications
Push alerts when Severe Weather Emergency Protocol activates in your chosen town.
📍
Town-specific alerts
Choose Eastbourne, Lewes, or both. Alerts are always relevant to where you are.
How it works

Simple by design

No account required for core features. Quick to pick up, built for anyone who needs it.

1
Download the app
Free on Android and iOS. No account needed to browse the map or directory.
2
Explore the map
Colour-coded service markers across Eastbourne and Lewes. Tap any pin for details.
3
Enable SWEP alerts
Opt in to push notifications and select your town. You'll be notified the moment SWEP activates.
4
Use it offline
Download the digital map for use without an internet connection.

SWEP alerts

When it matters most

Severe Weather Emergency Protocol is activated when temperatures drop dangerously low. WayMaker sends a push notification the moment SWEP is called — town by town, so the alert is always relevant.

Enable notifications and choose Eastbourne, Lewes, or both. You'll receive an alert as soon as emergency shelter opens — giving people time to reach safety.
